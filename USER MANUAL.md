Appendix A

User Manual

This manual provides authorized school personnel with comprehensive, step-by-step instructions on how to navigate and utilize the system. It is designed to help the School Head, Registrars, Grade Level Coordinators, and Class Advisers efficiently execute their tasks in compliance with Department of Education (DepEd) standards.

Overview of the System

This documentation focuses on EnrollPro, a core module within the Junior High School Information Management Portal. EnrollPro streamlines the beginning-of-school-year (BOSY) operations by centralizing learner enrollment, Special Curricular Program (SCP) admissions, automated sectioning, and the generation of official class rosters (School Form 1).

Note: In some accompanying screenshots, the names and numbers shown are sample data used strictly for demonstration and training purposes.

Figure 1: System Landing and Secure Login Page (School Head or System Administrator Side)
This is the main entry point for all authorized teaching and non-teaching personnel. Users must log in through this secure gateway to access their designated modules.

Step-by-Step Navigation:

1. Integrated Portal Overview:
The left panel displays the integrated systems available to the school. EnrollPro is the primary module highlighted here, dedicated to managing learner enrollment and official School Form 1 (SF1) class placements.

2. Personnel ID or TIN:
Click on this text field to enter your official DepEd Personnel ID or Tax Identification Number (TIN). This serves as your unique, secure username for the system.

3. Password & Sign In:
Enter your private password in this field. You can click the "eye" icon on the right side of the box to securely view your password and ensure there are no typos before clicking the solid color -> Sign In button to access the system.

### **Figure 2.a: Enrollment Operations Dashboard (School Head or System Administrator Side)**

Upon successful login during the Beginning of School Year (BOSY), the system redirects you to the Enrollment Operations Dashboard. This upper section serves as your central command center, providing real-time oversight and direct links to urgent administrative tasks.

**Step-by-Step Navigation:**

**1. Monitor Institutional Metrics:** Review the top red panel to immediately check the total enrollees, active personnel, initialized sections, and records requiring review.

**2. Process Walk-In SCP Admission:** Click the "Walk-In SCP Admission" button under Quick Actions to open the encoding form overlay. This allows you to immediately select a special program (e.g., STE) and input an applicant's LRN without navigating away from the dashboard.

**3. Process Walk-In Enrollment:** Click the "Walk-In Enrollment" button under Quick Actions to launch the enrollment form overlay. This lets you quickly select the learner's classification (New Entrant, Transferee, or Returnee) and encode their basic details to temporarily enroll them.

**4. Execute Auto Assign Sections:** Click the "Auto Assign Sections" button under Quick Actions to trigger the system's sorting algorithm. The system will instantly redirect you to the Section Assignment module.

**5. Review Pending Enrollments:** Check the "Pending Enrollment" alert card and click "Process Enrollment Records ->" to navigate to the queue of learner applications awaiting official Registrar approval.

**6. Manage Unsectioned Learners:** Check the "Unsectioned Learners" alert card and click "Assign Class Sections ->" to manually place approved learners into their specific classrooms.

**7. Track Missing Requirements:** Check the "Missing School Requirements" alert card and click "Review Missing Requirements ->" to identify learners lacking mandatory DepEd credentials, such as the SF9 or PSA Birth Certificate.

**8. View Curricular Rosters:** Scroll to the "Learners by Curricular Program" section and click "View Roster ->" under BEC or STE to access the specific masterlist for that academic track.

**9. Track Grade Level Demographics:** Review the "Enrollment Records by Grade Level" table to monitor the exact count of Continuing, New, Transferee, and Balik-Aral learners across Grades 7 through 10.

### **Figure 5.a: SCP Admission (Document Screening)**

The SCP Admission module allows the Registrar and Program Coordinators to evaluate applicants for Special Curricular Programs (such as STE, SPA, or SPS). The evaluation follows a strict, multi-stage screening process.

**Step-by-Step Navigation:**

**1. Select Program Category:** Click the dark red tabs at the top (e.g., `STE APPLICANTS`, `SPA APPLICANTS`) to view the applicants specific to that track.

**2. Evaluate Documentary Requirements:** Review the submitted paperwork for each applicant. Under the `PASSED REQUIREMENTS` column, click the dropdown menu and select `PASSED` if their documents are complete and meet the grade cut-off. Select `INCOMPLETE / FAILED` to immediately disqualify them.

**3. Save Progress:** Click the dark red `Save Results` button at the top right to lock in the initial screening statuses.

---

### **Figure 5.b: SCP Admission (Written Exam Encoding)**

Once an applicant passes the initial document screening, the system unlocks the next phase of the evaluation.

**Step-by-Step Navigation:**

**1. Encode Written Exam Scores:** Locate the `WRITTEN EXAM` column. Click the dropdown menu in the applicant's row to input their official entrance test score (e.g., 62, 60, 65).

**2. Save Exam Results:** Click the `Save Results` button to record the scores. The system will automatically mark applicants who did not take or pass the exam as disqualified.

---

### **Figure 5.c: SCP Admission (Interview & Automated Ranking)**

The final step of the evaluation involves the interview phase. After this, the system automatically sorts the applicants based on the available slots configured during the BOSY setup.

**Step-by-Step Navigation:**

**1. Encode Interview Results:** Under the `INTERVIEW` column, click the dropdown and select `PASSED` or `FAILED` for each remaining applicant.

**2. Review Automated Ranking:** Click `Save Results`. The system will automatically calculate the scores and sort the applicants into distinct categories:

* **QUALIFIED:** Top-ranking applicants who secured a slot (e.g., Luz G. Dela Cruz and Mark A. Garcia).


* **WAITLISTED:** Applicants who passed all screenings but did not make the top cut-off due to limited program slots (e.g., Camille A. Bautista).


* **UNQUALIFIED STE APPLICANTS:** Applicants who failed any stage of the screening (e.g., Carlo G. Ramos).



---

**Step-by-Step Navigation:**

**1. Initiate Roster Lock:** Click the red `Finalize & Lock Roster` button located at the top right corner of the table.

**2. Confirm Finalization:** A warning pop-up will appear. Read the confirmation prompt to ensure all data is correct, then click the dark red `FINALIZE & LOCK` button. This action locks the list and prevents any accidental score modifications.

The system will automatically promote the highest-ranking learner from the `WAITLISTED` category into the newly opened slot.
